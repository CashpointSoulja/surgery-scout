# Surgery Scout, explained simply

GP surgeries get slammed with phone calls at 8am. Lots of those calls never reach a human - the NHS's own numbers say so, practice by practice, every month.

QuantumLoopAI sells an AI receptionist called EMMA that answers every call on the first ring. Their sales reps need to know which surgeries miss the most calls, so they know who to ring first and what to say.

Surgery Scout takes the NHS's public September 2026 phone data for North East London - all 261 practices - and lines them up: who missed the most calls at 8-10am, how big they are, which network they belong to. Click any practice and it gives you the reason to call, a suggested first line, and three questions to ask.

It also prepares you for the two awkward questions ("didn't this struggle with accents?" and "is this even allowed under our contract?") with what was actually reported and how to answer honestly.

Everything on the page links back to the public NHS files it came from. It uses no patient data, it's not made by QuantumLoopAI, and the tiers are my own editorial cut of the numbers - a starting point for conversations, not proof anyone wants to buy.
